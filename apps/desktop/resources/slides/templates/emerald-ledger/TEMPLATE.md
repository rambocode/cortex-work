# Emerald Ledger

A magazine-cover business deck: emerald + navy + paper, double-rule masthead ornaments, and a bold Bodoni-style display serif.

## Best For

Anything that should feel like the front of a serious magazine, including but not limited to leadership readouts, planning-office reviews, and strategy briefings. The double-rule masthead ornament gives it editorial gravitas without making it stiff — also a great unexpected pick for product launches or research recaps that want to feel considered rather than corporate.

## Avoid For

Contexts that need to read as quiet, neutral, or institutionally restrained — the emerald field is too saturated to disappear into the background.

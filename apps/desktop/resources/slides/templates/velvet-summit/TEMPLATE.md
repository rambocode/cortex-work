# Velvet Summit

Deep plum-black canvas with champagne-gold serifs and cream type. Luxurious, ceremonial, high-end brand gravitas.

## Best For

Luxury and premium consumer brand pitches, hospitality and lifestyle ventures, flagship product reveals, and high-end partnership and sponsorship proposals. The plum-black velvet field with sparing champagne-gold serifs gives a fundraising pitch deck or business plan the ceremonial, high-net-worth gravitas it needs — restraint reads as wealth, and the gold only lands on the numbers and words that matter.

## Avoid For

Developer tools, infrastructure, or data-heavy operational reviews — the ceremonial gold works against utilitarian content. When a deck needs to feel fast, technical, or densely quantitative, this template's slow, luxurious tempo fights the message.

# Volt Pitch

Near-black charcoal canvas with electric-blue accents and cool white type. Sharp, confident, institutional-grade pitch energy.

## Best For

Fundraising pitch decks, business plans, product launches for tech/SaaS/infrastructure startups; investor updates and board decks that should feel sharp, credible, and quietly ambitious. Electric-blue accents carry the key numbers and growth story while the near-black field keeps the whole deck reading as institutional and composed.

## Avoid For

Playful consumer brands, lifestyle content, academic lectures — the cold precision reads as too corporate there. If the material wants warmth, whimsy, or a hand-made feel, reach for a lighter, softer template instead.

# Ember Launch

Deep charcoal canvas warmed by ember-orange accents and warm white type. Ambition with warmth — consumer launches that feel human.

## Best For

Consumer product and lifestyle brand pitch decks, D2C and food/beverage ventures, community-driven products, and launch keynotes that need warmth and human connection. The deep charcoal field keeps the deck grounded and confident while ember-orange accents carry the emotional beats — the hero numbers, the call to action, the moment you ask. Reach for it when the story is really about people: the ritual you're building, the community forming around it, the founders behind the brand.

## Avoid For

Cold institutional finance or compliance content — the ember warmth undercuts the formality those contexts demand. If the deck needs to read as neutral, austere, or regulatory, the human heat here works against you.

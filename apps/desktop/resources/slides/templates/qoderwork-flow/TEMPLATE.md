# QoderWork Flow

QoderWork native green-and-black presentation system with clean cards, strong contrast, and product-demo energy.

## Best For

QoderWork product updates, launch decks, internal readouts, product-demo narratives, technical strategy reviews, and any deck that should feel like it belongs inside QoderWork.

## Avoid For

Highly ornamental editorial decks or soft lifestyle storytelling where the product-workbench signal would feel too direct.

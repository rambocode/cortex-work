# Lime Venture

Ink-black canvas charged with lime-green accents and warm white type. Young, hungry, growth-story energy for new-generation founders.

## Best For

Seed/Series-A fundraising decks for consumer, creator-economy, and growth-stage startups; demo day presentations; growth reviews that lead with momentum and metrics. The system is built to make big numbers, hockey-stick curves, and bold single-claim statements land hard — every slide is engineered to project confidence and traction. Reach for it when the story is about speed, scale, and where the line goes next.

## Avoid For

Conservative industries (banking, legal, government) and formal advisory deliverables — the lime energy reads too casual there. Anything that needs a quiet, restrained, or scholarly register will fight the template's loud, momentum-forward personality.
